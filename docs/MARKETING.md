### Description

**Steel Trading Desk** is a local-first iPhone, iPad, and Mac discipline desk for discretionary traders who fade stretched VXX/VIX premium expansion.

The desk is one screen: a single command verb (NO TRADE / WAIT / CLEARED) on top of a four-gate sequence that has to clear in order before any entry is on the table. No signals, no autopilot, no fantasy fills.

**Free Full Desk, No Paywall:** The full VXX/VIX/VWAP workflow, embedded TradingView chart, prompt support, hard-exit takeover, and live-data integration are available without an in-app purchase.

**Key Features:**

*   **Four-Gate Sequence:** Volume + window, VXX/VIX ratio + divergence direction, VWAP / key level, and rejection pattern. The current blocker is always highlighted in amber.
*   **Tape Entry:** Fast manual input from ThinkOrSwim, Schwab, or your primary charting platform.
*   **Live Data (Optional):** Bring your own Finnhub, Polygon, Schwab, or Yahoo API key. The desk auto-populates VXX, VIX, volume, and VWAP every 30 seconds. Pattern, level, and news stay manual.
*   **Embedded Chart:** TradingView Advanced Chart (VWAP, SMA20, SMA50, Volume) directly inside the desk. 15-minute delayed disclosure built in.
*   **Hard-Exit Takeover:** Between 3:50 and 4:10 PM ET, the iPhone screen is replaced by a giant countdown to 15:55, your open position summary, and the three exit rules.
*   **Second Read:** Structured prompts to copy into the model of your choice for an outside check; never as the green light.
*   **Privacy First:** Zero telemetry. No analytics. No data sold or proxied. All keys live in the platform Keychain.

*Note: Educational tool for analysis and journaling. Trading involves risk. The app does not execute trades or provide financial advice.*
