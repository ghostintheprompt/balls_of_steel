### Description
**Steel Trading Desk** is a local-first, native macOS utility designed for discretionary traders focusing on SPY and VXX volatility strategies. 

Bridging the gap between chart analysis and rigorous trade management, Steel provides an elite interface for manual-entry validation and AI-assisted analysis.

**Full Desk, No Paywall:** The manual-entry desk, VXX/VIX/VWAP workflow, prompt support, and risk tools are available without an in-app purchase.

**Key Features:**
*   **Setup Validation:** Multi-factor checks for time windows, volume, VWAP, and volatility ratios.
*   **Signal Intelligence:** Direction-aware monitoring (WATCH, ENTRY, EXIT) with real-time institutional window alerts.
*   **Prompt Coach:** Pre-fill advanced analysis prompts with your live chart data to bridge your workflow with external AI tools.
*   **Risk Management:** Automated discipline warnings and hard-exit rules for market close.
*   **Privacy First:** Zero telemetry. All data stays on your local hardware.

*Note: This is an educational tool for analysis and journaling. Trading involves risk.*
