### Description
**Steel Trading Desk** is a local-first, native iPhone, iPad, and Mac utility for discretionary traders focused on VXX/VIX/VWAP entries.

Steel is not a broker, signal seller, or trading system. It is a fast discipline layer: enter the tape, check the gates in order, execute manually on your broker, and manage the exit.

**Full Desk, No Paywall:** The manual-entry desk, VXX/VIX/VWAP workflow, prompt support, and risk tools are available without an in-app purchase.

**Key Features:**
*   **VXX Sequence:** Volume/window, VXX/VIX ratio, VWAP/key level, and pattern gates in order.
*   **Tape Entry:** Fast manual input from ThinkOrSwim, Schwab, or your primary charting platform.
*   **Prompt Desk:** Structured prompts for a second read when useful; no autopilot.
*   **Risk Management:** Discipline warnings and hard-exit rules for market close.
*   **Privacy First:** Zero telemetry. All data stays on your local hardware.

*Note: This is an educational tool for analysis and journaling. Trading involves risk.*
