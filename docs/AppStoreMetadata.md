## App Description
**Steel Trading Desk** is a local-first, native macOS and iOS utility designed to bridge the gap between complex chart analysis and rigorous trade discipline. Built for traders who value systematic execution over emotional impulse.

**NO FANTASY FILLS.**
Most trading tools encourage over-trading. Steel Trading Desk does the opposite. It forces you to validate every setup against institutional flow, volume thresholds, and specific time windows before you ever touch your broker.

**KEY FEATURES:**
*   **VXX & SPY Dashboards:** High-contrast, "Boiler Room" aesthetic for maximum focus on the tape.
*   **Institutional Flow Monitoring:** Track volume ratios (200%-400%+) that signal major market moves.
*   **Prompt Coach:** Your personal AI assistant. Delivers live-data pre-filled prompts for ChatGPT/Claude at the exact moment you need them (Open Drive, Power Hour, Institutional Flow).
*   **Manual-First Discipline:** Validate signals manually to maintain "Skin in the Game."
*   **Zero Telemetry:** Your data never leaves your device. No accounts. No tracking. No bullshit.
